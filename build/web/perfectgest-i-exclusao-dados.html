Introduction

Last updated: 2 October 2026
PerfectGest is local-first. Agenda, quotes and cash are not sent automatically to PerfectGest servers. Website: https://perfectgestdev.com.

How to delete

- In the app: Settings → delete data on this device
- Android: Settings → Apps → PerfectGest → Storage → Clear data
- Uninstall the app

Without an external copy there is no recovery. Delete shared backups/PDFs where you saved them.

Telemetry

Generic Firebase events and a minimal technical record (Firestore). Email suporte@perfectgestdev.com (subject: Technical data deletion). Up to 15 business days. Local deletion does not by itself erase the Google-side record.
