What is PerfectGest?

A phone app for day-to-day management with local SQLite data: agenda (clients and suppliers), quotes, work orders, cash and PDF. Website: https://perfectgestdev.com. No in-app ads.

Does it work offline?

Yes for daily use. Analytics, Remote Config and technical Firestore may use the network.

Is there a subscription?

Operations can be used without a required subscription. An optional Pro plan is available on Google Play (price in the store).

How do I export data?

Settings → JSON ZIP export, or local backup. You choose who to share with.

How do I delete data?

Settings → delete data on this device, or uninstall. Telemetry: suporte@perfectgestdev.com.

Suggestions?

Company tab or suporte@perfectgestdev.com.
